# Discord announcement — ready after the GitHub draft is published

**STO Shakedown v0.6.5 is ready for testing!**

Pick your LCARS look: **Shakedown, Lower Decks, TNG, or Voyager**. The new Color Theme Select panel opens over the workspace, remembers your choice, and keeps the sidebar from growing when you open it. This update also includes our new app icon and Solans Labs branding.

Behind the scenes, Electron and Node are updated. All 27 automated tests passed, along with desktop security and saved-data checks. The dependency audit reported no known vulnerabilities, and the targeted Windows Defender scan found no threats. This remains an unsigned alpha build for testers.

**Updating?** Close your old version, back up its **data** folder, extract the new ZIP into a new folder, and copy your data folder beside the new **Shakedown.exe** before launching. Keep your old copy until you've checked your saved ships and runs.

**Download:** https://github.com/fukenny/sto-build-parser/releases/tag/v0.6.5

Please share any theme, readability, window-sizing, or saved-data issues you find. Development is moving quickly right now; as we get closer to production, updates will slow down and group changes into larger, more thoroughly tested releases.
